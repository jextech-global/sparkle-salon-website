Our Story

Sparkle Salon is a full-service beauty salon located in Moore, Idaho. We believe that everyone deserves access to top-quality beauty services without having to travel to the big city.

Our talented team of stylists and beauty professionals — including the beloved Belle and Kricket — are dedicated to helping you look and feel your absolute best. From nails and waxing to hair color and permanent makeup, we offer a complete range of services.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our website, book an appointment, or contact us, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Name and contact details (phone number, email address)
  • Appointment preferences and service selections
  • Any additional information you provide in forms or messages

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Schedule and manage your appointments
  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Send appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Improve our services and customer experience

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your information is only used internally to provide you with the best possible service at Sparkle Salon.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use basic cookies to improve your browsing experience. These cookies do not collect personal information and are used solely for website functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party services such as Google Maps. These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request correction of inaccurate information

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
